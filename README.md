# Extended Weapon Customization - xsScythe
Adds blades from the Combat Blade, Heavy Sword, Duelling Sword, and Devil's Claw Sword to your Weapon Customization menu.
You'll find them in the Head attachment slot of your Force staves.
Transform your staves into scythes – and become just like a Necromancer from Diablo or a maniacal Psyker.
Feel like a reaper of souls in the Warhammer hell!
Let the harvest begin!

# +++Manual Installation:
- Extract the mod into your MODS folder.
  - For example: ..\Steam\steamapps\common\Warhammer 40,000 DARKTIDE\mods         
- Add "**extended_weapon_customization_xsscythe**" to **mod_load_order.txt** manually or with my **Servo-Modquisitor**﻿
- Launch the game and enjoy!
